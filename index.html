Calo UK MP Performance – Strategic Review

The UK MP continues to demonstrate resilience and operational maturity, Despite the temporary spike in October, both Quality and Logistics have returned to a strong downward trajectory, with clear visibility toward achieving our Q1 2026 targets,

Operational Discipline

UK teams executed rapid, targeted corrective actions with measurable impact, stabilising performance and accelerating month‑on‑month improvement.

Strategic Outlook

Calo UK MP is firmly positioned to hit both Quality and Logistics complaint‑rate targets within Q1 2026.

Path to Target by March 2026

Calo UK is on track to reach the Quality (0.025%) and Logistics (0.008%) ambitions by March 2026. Achieving this requires disciplined follow‑through on the improvements already in motion. The next eight weeks will focus on operational execution across core levers:

  1. Stabilising Process Controls
    The steepest improvements occurred when process adherence tightened post‑October. March readiness depends on maintaining strict daily checks, reinforcing SOP compliance across all shifts, and ensuring corrective actions are consistently applied and sustained.
  2. Targeted Root‑Cause Elimination
    January data highlights a small number of recurring drivers across both food‑item and delivery complaints. Each criteria is being addressed through structured root‑cause analysis and rapid countermeasure trials to eliminate issues at source.

Outlook

With complaint rates already halved from baseline and month‑on‑month reductions holding steady, Calo UK is on credible trajectory to meet both targets by March, priority now is consistency, sustaining controls and rapid‑response mechanisms that have driven the last three months of improvement.
